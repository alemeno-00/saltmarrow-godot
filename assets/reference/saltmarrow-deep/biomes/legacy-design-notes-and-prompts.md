# Fresh biome mockups — town-led reset

Method: built-in ImageGen. These are approval mockups, not screenshots of implemented gameplay or production tilesets. The attached town image is the sole visual reference. No previous dungeon art is used as a reference. Game files, levels and town assets have not been deleted or changed in this design-preview stage.

Shared goals: one coherent architectural language; the town's pixel-art materials and lighting; clear traversable planes and solid wall masses; purposeful props; readable landmarks; a consistent small adventurer for scale. The eventual implementation needs new modular artwork and room layouts derived from the approved design, not a flattened mockup pasted into the game.

## rotwater-sewers

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: APPROVAL MOCKUP for a redesigned Saltmarrow Deep playable biome, not a finished game asset or tileset. Use ONLY the attached town image as the visual style reference. Match its beautiful cohesive detailed dark-fantasy pixel art: finely clustered pixels, hand-laid irregular stone, thick believable masonry, weathered structural supports, careful material shading, architectural depth and warm amber lights in cold darkness. SAME artist and same world as that town. Start from scratch; do not copy any old dungeon design. Show a complete playable environment, not a tile sheet or a cinematic poster. Slightly overhead orthographic gameplay cutaway with an axis-aligned square floor plan, upright north-facing architecture and low/cutaway foreground walls; no rotated diamond grid. Underlying 32px tactical grid but NO drawn grid lines. A believable composition of several connected spaces and a memorable landmark. Walkable paths must be immediately obvious: moderately lighter, quieter floor planes, substantially darker thick wall masses, crisp boundary shadows, distinct edges to pits/water. Not a washed-out grey overlay. Reserve brightest highlights for amber lights, focal objects and tiny environmental accents. Clean open traversable space occupies at least half the image. Props are arranged purposefully around room margins, never sprinkled on every tile. One small recognisable human adventurer with an amber lantern on the main path provides game scale; architecture and props consistently scaled. Deep dark unwalkable void outside structures. No UI, no HUD, no text, no labels, no typography. Landscape 3:2 composition. This is a visual mockup for user review before implementing or deleting game content. Rotwater Sewers: a subterranean Victorian-gothic drainage complex below that coastal town. Three connected stone platforms flank a dark flooded channel; a narrow maintenance bridge crosses it. Main focal landmark is an immense rusted pumping engine integrated into thick masonry beside a giant barred drainage arch. Secondary flooded saint niche with a few amber candles, a leaking pipe, valve wheel and old workshop alcove. Blue-grey wet stone floors with restrained reflections and some broken patches, dark moss-stained walls, rusty copper machinery, dark teal water with small ripples. Warm amber lanterns mark the dry route; rusty machinery is detailed but never clutters the path. Architecture has the same stone courses, arches and weathering as the reference town. Danger lies in the water and broken service tunnel, clearly separate from usable paths. Avoid excessive glowing water, repetitive identical bricks, decorative floor noise and anonymous square rooms.

The Blooming Deep: an ancient subterranean monastic conservatory/cavern overtaken by a beautiful sinister fungal ecosystem. A clear winding stone causeway connects two old masonry terraces over a shadowy spore basin. A ruined gothic arch grown through with roots shelters a luminous mycelial heart as the main landmark. A few enormous turquoise mushroom canopies form a natural cathedral overhead; pale gold gills and sparse purple growth. Keep the base building stone and support language exactly like the town reference, visibly being consumed by organic roots. The usable floor is muted mossy stone and dark brown fungal soil, lighter than the deep root-enclosed wall masses, quiet enough to see the adventurer. Teal/turquoise glow concentrated in mushroom caps and the heart, not covering the whole floor. Amber lanterns left by previous expeditions mark a safe route. Dark gaps between terraces, root bridges, a shallow spore pond and one side shrine. Avoid neon carpets, mushrooms on every tile, generic green cave and floor/wall camouflage.

The Salt Pits: an immense abandoned salt excavation beneath the same gothic coastal town. A broad pale salt-dusted excavation terrace and a narrow timber bridge span a deep black shaft. A massive blue-white salt pillar partly encases a buried saint; the main landmark stands beside an older gothic mining arch cut into black rock. Structural timber, a rope lift, cart rails and a compact abandoned workers camp are purposeful secondary details. Same beautifully consistent stone, wood and lantern rendering as the town reference. Usable ground is muted pale salt on grey stone, visibly separate from dark excavated walls and the black shaft. Show clear routes, strong material boundaries, repair history and dramatic depth, while maintaining square-grid-compatible flat 2D gameplay space. Avoid uniformly bright white scenery, icy fantasy palace, excessive crystal clutter and repetitive flat grey mine corridors.
