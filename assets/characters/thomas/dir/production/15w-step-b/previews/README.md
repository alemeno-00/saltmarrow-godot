# Complete south walk previews

These are mechanical composites of the final eight diffuse/normal frames, not engine screenshots. South-facing 80 × 80 source frames at 10 fps (0.8 s per loop), nominal root (40,72). Room grounding/shadows/lighting are left to the game; no painted shadow is baked into the sprite. All resizing is nearest neighbour. GIF review palettes may approximate room colours; runtime PNGs retain exact palettes.
