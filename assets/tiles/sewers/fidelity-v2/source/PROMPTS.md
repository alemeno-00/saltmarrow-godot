Medium slate grey, charcoal joints and wet browns; match the reference's crafted weathered stone. Do not bake orange light, broad cast shadows or shiny white spots. Readable hard-edged pixel clusters; no noisy flat texture or soft painting. The modules should have different strong stone SHAPES and scales that read in game, not just different noise patterns. No text, no border, no thick dark outline around each rectangular asset, no water. Keep clear transparent gaps between the six module rectangles.
