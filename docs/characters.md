# Characters for art

How the cast looks and behaves, for the artist. Copied from the shared design doc; this file is the reference ChatGPT works from.

## Style notes for everyone

- A cold, wet, gothic fantasy harbour town at the end of a long war. Swords, staves and lanterns; **no guns**, and no real-world country or war.
- Wool, oilskin, leather, worn-through elbows: greatcoats, waistcoats, aprons, shawls, flat caps.
- Palette matches the town: slate blues, charcoal, wet browns, small warm amber accents. Each character has one signature colour so they read at a glance.
- **The star's touch** looks the same on everyone: a pearly sheen in the eyes, a faint inner light under the skin at the throat or hands, salt crystals at the edges of clothing. Use it sparingly.
- Quiet horror: people look ordinary until you look twice. No tentacles everywhere.

## Thomas Wren, the hero

- **Age and build:** 28. Tall, lean and underfed. A soldier's posture that slips into a stoop when tired.
- **Face:** long, hollow cheeks, restless grey eyes. Dark brown army-short hair growing out unevenly, two weeks of stubble. A healed blade scar from his left cheekbone to a notch in his left ear.
- **Wear:** his army greatcoat, dyed dark to hide the regiment colours, mismatched buttons. Wool trousers, wrapped shins, cracked campaign boots. A faded red scarf Ellen knitted: **his signature colour**.
- **Carries:** a canvas satchel of Ellen's letters tied with string, a hooded campaign lantern on his belt, and whatever weapon his build uses (maul, knives, staff, flail).
- **Personality:** quiet, watchful, dry. Polite to everyone, trusts no one. Flinches at bells and sudden noise. Gentle with children and the sick. Stubborn past sense.
- What he did in the war is never stated, so any weapon or magic fits him.

## Ellen Wren, his sister

- **Age and build:** 22. Small and slight, quick hands. Freckled, a wide crooked smile, brown eyes, copper hair in a loose untidy braid (**copper** is her colour).
- **Personality:** warm, teasing, braver than she looks. Sings when nervous.

| Look | When | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ill | Opening letter, flashbacks | Pale with a fever flush, dark circles. Grey wool dress, heavy oatmeal shawl, handkerchief in hand. |
| Healed, in the chapel | Later letters, visions | Glowing with too much health. Hair loose and smooth. White chapel smock, a small black star-metal pin shaped like a falling star. A first faint pearly sheen in her eyes. Smiles all the time. |
| In the star | The final descent | Seen through deep water: a silhouette in white light, hair floating upward, eyes closed and calm, the light coming from inside her. |

## Sister Agathe

- **Age and build:** 64. Tall, broad-shouldered, very upright.
- **Face:** weathered and kind, deep laugh lines, pale blue eyes with a pearly sheen visible only in low light. Rough, scrubbed hands.
- **Wear:** grey habit with a **sea-blue** scapular (her colour), the hem stained white with salt, plain white wimple. Around her neck, where a cross would hang, a smooth pendant of black star-metal.
- **Carries:** a brass hand-lamp, always lit.
- **Personality:** warm, motherly, completely certain. Never raises her voice, remembers every name, touches your hand when she speaks. Believes the star is an angel and that she is saving everyone she sends to it.
- **Three versions:** Act 1 almost no sign; Act 3 the inner light shows at her throat when she prays and salt crusts her cuffs; final, her eyes shine fully pearl and light shows through her hands, serene and smiling, still kind.
