# Skill trees

Every tree follows the same pattern:

- 10 nodes in four rows: 5 abilities and 5 passives, with the **capstone** at the bottom.
- Take a node by owning one connected to it in the row above.
- Abilities roll 2d10 + a stat. Results are written as three tiers, **worst / middle / best**.

## Scope

- 13 trees, 130 nodes, 65 abilities.
- **Two kinds of points:**
  - **Level points**, 1 per level, up to about 20 in a life. They're spent on **spell schools and the utility trees** (Stealth, Survival, Warfare).
  - **Mastery points**, earned by **landing hits with a weapon type**. They can only be spent in that weapon's tree.
- **How trees open:**
  - A **weapon tree** opens after about 10 hits with that weapon type, or instantly from its manual.
  - A **spell school** opens only from its tome.
  - **Reading a manual or tome gives one free point** in that tree.
- **Tuning target:** your main weapon reaches its capstone around the middle of a good run; a second weapon gets about halfway; a third barely starts. This encourages one main weapon and a backup.
- **Numbers in the game now:**
  - Experience: 1 per minion, 4 per standard enemy. Level 2 at 5, then 15, 30, 50 (5 × level × (level + 1) / 2).
  - Each level: +1 level point and +3 Stamina; a stat point every third level.
  - Mastery: a mace point every 8 landed hits.
- **The Fury starts with** Skull Crack and Weight Behind It. (Other backstories come later.)
- Shatter and Sunder wait for armour and items.
- Each node costs 1 point. Take a node by owning one connected to it in the row above, so a capstone takes at least 4 points.
- Points stay spent for the rest of a life; death resets everything.
- **Anyone can use any weapon.** The basic attack needs no points. Weapon abilities and passives need that weapon in hand.
- **Combos should key off what you're holding or standing in, more than points in a second tree.** For example, Swinging Censer needs a flail in hand, not points in Flails.
- **Backstories give two starting nodes**, so every life opens with options.
- **Special weapons carry an ability of their own.** For example, a star-metal maul has a Star ability that risks Phenomena; a chapel censer-flail has a Faith one.
- **Later, maybe:** hidden pairing passives that open when you invest in two particular trees. For example, Maces + Storm adds a lightning crack when you push an enemy into a wall.

## Injuries (all weapons and enemies)

- Each ability names a body part and an injury chance for each tier, shown on its card.
- Injuries last until treated:
  - **Head:** dazed more easily; worse vision.
  - **Arms:** weaker attacks; a chance to drop the weapon.
  - **Legs:** slowed.
  - **Torso:** bleeding.
- The same rule applies when enemies hit Thomas.

### Terms

- **Dazed:** only one of move, maneuver or main action on its turn.
- **Slowed:** speed 2.
- **Prone:** on the ground. Standing up costs movement.
- **Edge:** +2 to the roll.

## Swords (Might or Agility)

The traditional longsword: a **jack of all trades**. One blade can cut (slashing, bleeding), thrust through armour gaps (piercing) or strike with the hilt (crushing, daze). It also has the duellist's parry and riposte. Dual-wielding belongs to Daggers.

**Bleeding:** loses 2 Stamina at the start of each of its turns until treated or shaken off.

**Triggered abilities** are used on an enemy's turn, in response to its attack.

## Daggers (Agility)

The killer in the dark: **stealth, crits, bleeding, dual-wielding, and winning the fight before it starts.** The weakest weapon in a straight brawl.

### Rules this tree uses

- **Crits:** a natural 19 or 20 on the 2d10 gives the best tier plus the crit cinematic. Daggers widen the range.
- **Unaware:** an enemy that hasn't spotted you, or can't see you. Attacks against it have an edge.
- **Preparation abilities belong only to Daggers.**
  - They're used while exploring, unseen, before a fight.
  - They cost no Resolve. Instead they cost a few turns, some fatigue, and a quiet noise check. A failed check can alert enemies.
  - What you set up is waiting when the fight begins.
- **Unconscious:** out of the fight; takes no turns. Hurting it wakes it, but that hit is an automatic crit.

## Rules for all spells

- **Each school gives a free basic spell** once learned, like a weapon's basic attack.
- Bigger spells cost **Resolve**, use the **main action**, and need **one free hand** and **line of sight**.
- Rolls are 2d10 + the school's stat, with three tiers and body-part injury chances, the same as weapons.
- **The environment matters.** Water, darkness, armour and the enemy's nature change what a spell does.
- **Friendly fire is on.** Areas, chains and electrified water hit Thomas too if he's inside them.
- Storm, Faith and Mind are safe. Only the Star risks Phenomena.

## Faith (Presence)

The old church's faith, the one Agathe abandoned. Its four jobs: **healing, crowd control, survivability, and the Star.** The Star creates Dread; Faith consumes it.

- **Star-touched:** anything the star has changed (the Faithful, the Drowned, Hollow Faith, Carrion Brood). Faith is strongest against them, and weak against ordinary threats.
- **Partner weapon:** flails. Their Faith combos only need a flail **in hand**.

### The Star changes Thomas

As Attunement rises, Thomas shows the same signs as Agathe, on his sprite and portrait, and the town notices.

| Attunement | Look | The town |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 | No sign | Normal |
| 3–5 | **Pearl eyes**, a faint sheen | Agathe is warmer and calls him "child". Nell asks what he's been doing down there. |
| 6–8 | Plus **light under the skin** at the throat and hands | Rook charges more for anything ordinary and less for star-metal. Children hide. Bram serves him last. |
