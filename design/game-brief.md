**One-sentence pitch:** A gothic, Lovecraftian roguelike in which a soldier home from the war fights his way down through the tunnels under a cliff town, in slow, heavy Draw Steel fights, looking for the sister a fallen star took.

## Core loop
The full game: take contracts and buy supplies in town, go down, explore turn by turn while hunger, thirst, fatigue, pain, injuries and Dread wear Thomas down, fight, grow, come back up on foot or by a shaft landing, and go again. Death regenerates the world; only the story, the town's memory and Marks of the star stay.

The MVP keeps the dive at the heart of it:
- Explore a generated level quietly. Being seen freezes the world into turns.
- Fight with 2d10 power rolls. Every fight costs Stamina, Recoveries and injuries; the enemy side grows Malice each round.
- Grow stronger: kills give experience (1 per minion, 4 per standard enemy) and mace hits give mastery points (1 per 8 hits), both spent on the Maces tree.
- Take the stairs deeper, keeping everything learned, and push on until you win or die.

## Player goal & fail state — what "working" looks like
- **Win:** beat the boss at the bottom of the Sewers. In the full game, the win is finding Ellen at the star.
- **Fail:** Thomas dies, which happens if his Stamina drops below minus half his maximum, or he bleeds out while dying. The run ends and everything he learned is wiped.

**Who it's for / what they feel:** Players of tactical games and roguelikes who want every fight to feel heavy and dangerous, and to get stronger the deeper they go.

**Art & audio direction:** Dark pixel art in cold slate, charcoal and wet browns with small amber lights. Graphic wounds that stay on screen, and no floating numbers. Audio isn't chosen yet.

**Reference game:** Stoneshard — skill trees that grow by use, and costly, grounded fights on a dungeon dive. The first version keeps one weapon tree and the dive, and drops survival and the overworld. The fight rules come from the Draw Steel tabletop RPG.
