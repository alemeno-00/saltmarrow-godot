# Quick Design Spec: Sneaking (and the dagger build it opens)

**Type**: New Small System
**Status**: APPROVED by Julian, 2026-10-30, with the decisions at the end. The brief's scope now includes Daggers,
the Shadow backstory and choosing a backstory (MVP item 11).
**Scope**: Moving unseen while exploring, being spotted gradually instead of at once, and starting fights on your
own terms. Daggers (`docs/trees.md`) and the Shadow backstory get their footing; the full Stealth tree is written
separately.
**Date**: 2026-10-30
**Estimated Implementation**: about a week, after fights in metres.

## Overview

Julian, after the first playtest: "eventually a sneaking mechanic, like Baldur's Gate 3, so builds that use daggers
or other sneaking mechanics can use it." Now that the world runs in real time, enemies can *look* somewhere. Each
enemy sees in a cone in front of it; being in that cone fills a meter rather than starting a fight at once. Sneaking
(crouched, slow) and darkness fill it slower. Noise turns heads. A fight started from hiding gives Thomas a surprise
round, which is what Daggers is built around.

## Core Rules

1. **Sneak** (C toggles): Thomas crouches, moves at 50 % speed, and makes little noise.
2. **The lantern** has three states: **lit**, **shuttered** (dimmed to a small pool; anyone can use it, and it
   helps a little) and **out** (no light at all: best for stealth). Putting it out is quick; **relighting** it
   takes time and makes a small flare of light and noise. Oil and what the dark does to Thomas are in the lantern
   spec (still to write).
3. **Vision cones.** Each enemy sees 10.5 m in a 100° cone the way it faces, and 2 m all round (it notices anything
   that close). The cone is drawn on the floor, BG3-style, for enemies within 15 m of Thomas. Walls block it.
   Patrols and idle enemies turn to look about, so cones sweep.
4. **Being spotted is a meter**, not a switch. While Thomas is in a cone the enemy's meter fills: faster close up,
   faster in lantern light (his own lantern counts: lit is worst, shuttered helps, out is best), slower
   while sneaking, much slower in the dark. Half full: the enemy is **suspicious**
   ("?" over it): it stops and turns toward him, then comes to look. Full: **spotted** ("!"): the fight starts. Out
   of sight, the meter drains.
5. **Noise.** Walking is heard 4 m away, sneaking 1 m, wading twice as far, a fight 15 m. A heard noise makes an
   enemy suspicious and sends it to look at the spot.
6. **Unaware.** An enemy that hasn't spotted Thomas is unaware. Attacks against an unaware enemy have an edge
   (Daggers' rule), and Backstab needs it (or a flank).
7. **Surprise.** Attacking from sneak starts the fight with Thomas acting first, and every enemy that hadn't spotted
   him is **surprised** for round 1: no free strikes, and attacks against it have an edge (Draw Steel's surprise).
8. **Hiding in a fight** (a maneuver): out of every enemy's sight and not adjacent to one, Thomas can hide; enemies
   lose track of where he is (they go to where they last saw him) and he becomes unaware-to-them until he attacks
   or is found. This is how a dagger build gets Backstab mid-fight; Mind's Veil does it without needing cover.
9. **Outside a fight, Daggers' tools work on unaware enemies:** Throw to put out a lantern (the area goes dark) or
   clatter a dagger off a far wall (one enemy wanders off alone to look), Whisper to knock one out silently, and the
   preparations (Study the Mark, Cut the Straps, Tripwire).
10. **The Shadow backstory** starts with daggers and points in Stealth; its mastery comes from striking an enemy that
   can't see Thomas (`docs/story.md`).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Sneaking behind a patrol's cone, in the dark, never fills a meter; walking through its cone in lantern light
      starts a fight within half a second.
- [ ] A suspicious enemy turns, comes to look, and goes back if it finds nothing.
- [ ] A thrown dagger off a far wall pulls exactly one enemy away to investigate.
- [ ] Attacking from sneak gives a surprise round: Thomas first, surprised enemies give no free strikes.
- [ ] Hiding mid-fight works only out of every enemy's sight, and enables Backstab.
- [ ] Playtest: crossing a room unseen feels tense, and being spotted never feels unfair (the cones and meters
      always showed it coming).

## Decisions (Julian, 2026-10-30)

1. **Daggers and the Shadow backstory are in the first version.**
2. **Thomas chooses his backstory at the start of each life** (Fury or Shadow for now).
3. **The lantern:** shuttering only dims it, and any build can use that. For stealth, putting it out is better, but
   relighting it takes time. Julian also asked for **lantern oil as a resource** and for **the dark to affect Thomas
   over time**: these get their own spec (the lantern and the dark), written next to this one.
